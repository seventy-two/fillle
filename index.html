How to play

  1. A patron walks up and orders five beers. Each beer has its own glass, and the glasses are waiting on the bar.
  2. Pick a tap (click it, or press Q, W, E, R, T). Each tap shows its glass shape underneath.
  3. Press and hold a glass (or 1–5) to pour that beer into it. Let go to stop. Stop at the line.
  4. Each beer pours at its own speed, changing every day. Perfect pours take 8 seconds; you have 15, from your first tap or pour.
  5. Each glass scores up to 100, losing 2 points per 1% off the line. The wrong beer, or two beers in one glass, scores 0.
  6. One go a day. Finish early with Done.
