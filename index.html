How to play

  1. Press and hold a cup to pour into it, or hold its number key (1–5). Let go to stop.
  2. Stop at the line. Each cup pours at its own speed, and the speeds change every day. Everyone gets the same cups.
  3. Perfect pours take 28 seconds in total. You have 30, starting from your first pour.
  4. Each cup scores up to 100: you lose 2 points for every 1% over or under the line. Pour past the rim and it spills.
  5. One go a day. Finish early with Done.
